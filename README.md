# Maya's Story: Living with Leigh Syndrome

An immersive, story-driven educational game for AP Biology presentations about Leigh Syndrome. Experience life through Maya's eyes - a 16-year-old with a mitochondrial disease - and learn the science behind this rare genetic disorder.

## About the Game

This isn't just a game - it's an interactive narrative experience that combines storytelling with education. Players walk through Maya's daily life while learning about cellular respiration, ATP production, and how genetic mutations cause Leigh Syndrome.

## The Story

**Meet Maya**: A 16-year-old student living with Leigh Syndrome, caused by a mutation in Complex IV of the electron transport chain. Through five interactive chapters, you'll experience her world and understand the biology behind her struggles.

## Game Features

### 📖 Story-Driven Narrative
- **Interactive Dialogue**: Conversations with Maya and her doctor
- **Character Development**: Experience Maya's perspective through her thoughts and choices
- **Emotional Impact**: Feel the real-life challenges of living with a mitochondrial disease
- **Educational Integration**: Science seamlessly woven into the story

### 🎮 Five Interactive Chapters

#### Chapter 1: Maya's Morning
- **Gameplay**: Make choices for Maya as she gets ready for school
- **Mechanic**: Energy management - every decision costs energy
- **Learning**: Experience how simple tasks become exhausting with impaired ATP production
- **Biology Concept**: Energy demands of daily activities

#### Chapter 2: Inside Maya's Cells
- **Gameplay**: Click-based game representing mitochondrial function
- **Mechanic**: Collect nutrients (glucose, oxygen) while avoiding toxins and mutations
- **Learning**: See how damaged mitochondria struggle to produce ATP
- **Biology Concept**: Cellular respiration at the mitochondrial level

#### Chapter 3: The Doctor Visit
- **Gameplay**: Dialogue sequence with Dr. Martinez
- **Mechanic**: Story-driven learning through conversation
- **Learning**: Understand the electron transport chain and Complex IV mutation
- **Biology Concept**: ETC malfunction, genetic mutations, and Leigh Syndrome pathology

#### Chapter 4: A Day at School
- **Gameplay**: Navigate school scenarios with declining energy
- **Mechanic**: Decision-making with consequences based on energy levels
- **Learning**: Experience the social and physical challenges of chronic illness
- **Biology Concept**: Energy deficiency impact on high-demand activities

#### Chapter 5: Understanding the Electron Transport Chain
- **Gameplay**: Interactive drag-and-drop puzzle
- **Mechanic**: Place molecules (NADH, FADH₂, O₂, etc.) in correct ETC complexes
- **Learning**: Master the exact biochemical pathway of ATP production
- **Biology Concept**: Complete electron transport chain mechanism

## Educational Objectives

Students will learn:
- **Cellular Biology**: Mitochondrial structure and function
- **Biochemistry**: ATP production through the electron transport chain
- **Genetics**: How mutations disrupt cellular processes
- **Physiology**: Energy demands of different organ systems
- **Medical Science**: Symptoms, diagnosis, and impact of Leigh Syndrome
- **Empathy**: Understanding life with a chronic mitochondrial disease

## How to Play

1. **Open `index.html`** in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
2. **No installation required** - runs entirely in the browser
3. **Start Maya's Story** from the main menu
4. **Make choices** that affect Maya's energy levels
5. **Experience all 5 chapters** to complete the journey
6. **Or** use Chapter Select to jump to specific topics

## For Presentations

### Suggested Use:
1. **Introduction (5 min)**: Present Leigh Syndrome basics
2. **Chapter 1-2 (10 min)**: Play through Maya's morning + cellular level
3. **Discussion (5 min)**: Class discusses what they noticed
4. **Chapter 3 (5 min)**: Doctor visit explains the science
5. **Chapter 4-5 (10 min)**: Complete school day + ETC puzzle
6. **Conclusion (5 min)**: Discuss takeaways and Q&A

### Key Discussion Questions:
- How did making choices for Maya feel different than learning from a textbook?
- What surprised you most about the energy costs of daily activities?
- How does Complex IV mutation affect the entire electron transport chain?
- What symptoms of Leigh Syndrome connect to ATP deficiency?
- How might understanding the molecular basis help develop treatments?
